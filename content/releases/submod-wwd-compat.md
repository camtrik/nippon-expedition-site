<!-- lang: zh -->

这个 mod 只有同时订阅了 **[与龙同行](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2789903784)** 的玩家才需要装。

- 2026-10-06 上线。玉海舰队与震旦各派系可以互相合邦，包括尼朋的奥本氏、山陽氏。其他震旦派系之间的合邦仍按与龙同行的规则。
    - ⚠️ 本包需要与龙同行才能运行，不装与龙同行会无法进入游戏。开档前决定装不装，不要中途退订与龙同行。
- 2026-10-06 玉海舰队可以招募与龙同行开局即开放的 14 支精英部队。
    - 这一部分原来在震旦机制兼容包里，现在移到这里。由与龙同行自己的任务链解锁的部队没有包含在内。
- 2026-10-06 玉海舰队不受「天朝局势」影响。
- 2026-10-06 顶栏显示军功；长枪武士、铁炮武士、雷刃旗本等 10 种自制兵可以用军功升级。

<!-- lang: en -->

You only need this mod if you are also subscribed to **[Walking with Dragons (DEER24 Cathay)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2789903784)**.

- 2026-10-06 Released. The Jade Sea Fleet and every Cathay faction can confederate each other again, including the Okumoto and Sanyo Clans on Nippon. Confederation between other Cathay factions still follows Walking with Dragons' rules.
    - ⚠️ This patch requires Walking with Dragons; the game will not start without it. Decide before starting a campaign, and do not unsubscribe from Walking with Dragons mid-campaign.
- 2026-10-06 The Jade Sea Fleet can recruit the 14 Regiments of Renown that Walking with Dragons makes available from the start of the campaign.
    - This part used to live in the Cathay Mechanics Compatibility Patch and has moved here. Regiments that Walking with Dragons unlocks through its own quest chains are not included.
- 2026-10-06 The Jade Sea Fleet is exempt from 天朝局势 (the State of the Celestial Empire).
- 2026-10-06 The top bar shows 军功 (Merits), and 10 of the mod's own units — Yari Samurai, Teppo Samurai, Thunderblade Hatamoto and more — can be upgraded with them.
