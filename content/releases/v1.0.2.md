- 尼朋的建筑与地标现在会提供驻军。
- 侍所链与武者屋形链各新增一级入门建筑，长枪武士现在在 2 级定居点就能招募。

### 调整
- 雷刃旗本与麒麟的招募回合数由 1 回合增加到 2 回合。

### Added
- Nippon's buildings and landmarks now provide garrison units.
- The Samurai Hall and Warrior's Manor chains each gain an entry-level building, so Yari Samurai can be recruited from a level 2 settlement.

### Changed
- Thunderblade Hatamoto and Kirin now take 2 turns to recruit, up from 1.
