这个 mod 只有同时订阅了 **[WH3 Tech-tree Combination](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3117083206)** 的玩家才需要装。中文玩家还需要订阅 [WH3 Tech-tree Combination 简体中文](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3787849519)。

- 2026-09-08 上线。玉海舰队的科技面板多出 WH3 Tech-tree Combination 给震旦做的那条支线，73 个节点，加成同时作用于舰队的自制兵种。

<!-- lang: en -->

You only need this mod if you are also subscribed to **[WH3 Tech-tree Combination](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3117083206)**.

- 2026-09-08 Released. The Jade Sea Fleet's technology panel gains the branch WH3 Tech-tree Combination adds for Cathay — 73 nodes, with the bonuses applying to the fleet's own custom units as well.
