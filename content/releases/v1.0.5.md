### 新增
- 新增「极东航道」：为适应新地图，为玉海舰队增加三条快速航线。

### 适配 9.0 与 IEE 新尼朋地图
- 玉海舰队开局位置调整。
- 远征同伴触发位置：瑟兰娜改为修验寺，立花直虎改为邪见。
- 胜利目标按新地图重新划定。
- 领主搬家位置：格里姆格·铁皮改到灵道大社，『流放者』斯卡布兰德改到浮世。
- 其他震旦派系的武士链改为攻占开闻解锁，神社链改为攻占灵道大社解锁。
- 水师提督：修验寺与钢铁要塞对玉海舰队视同主要港口；开局提醒任务可以直接点城名定位。
- 玉海舰队改用原版震旦科技树，「[WH3 Tech-tree Combination](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3117083206)」装上即可直接生效，科技树兼容包随之作废。

### 调整
- 纱代的神兽召来：麒麟改为持续 3 分钟。
- 玉海舰队对冰原、魔法森林、荒原、海洋气候改为适宜。
- 沧溟巨炮：「骤雨」每次开火改为消耗 2 发弹药（每轮炮弹数不变），「奔流」不受影响。
- 神社升到 T3 就有巫女的招募名额。

### 修复
- 修复纱代召唤的麒麟没有天雷回响和天国闪电的问题。
- 修复舰队瞭望塔升到二级后无法招募丹鼎师的问题。

### Added
- New **Far East Seaways**: to suit the new map, the Jade Sea Fleet gets three fast sea routes.

### Updated for 9.0 and IEE's new Nippon map
- The Jade Sea Fleet's starting position has moved.
- Companion triggers: Serena now starts at Shugengan, Tachibana Naotora at Jakken.
- Victory objectives have been redrawn for the new map.
- Lord relocations: Grimgor Ironhide now lands at The Great Shrine, Skarbrand the Exiled at Ukiyo.
- For other Cathay factions, the Samurai Hall line now unlocks by capturing Kaimon, and the Shrine line by capturing The Great Shrine.
- Fleet Admiral: Shugengan and Tetsu Fortress count as major ports for the Jade Sea Fleet, and the opening reminder mission lets you click a city name to jump to it.
- The Jade Sea Fleet now uses the vanilla Cathay tech tree, so [WH3 Tech-tree Combination](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3117083206) works as soon as it is installed. The tech-tree compatibility patch is retired.

### Changed
- Sayo's Summon Divine Beast: Kirin now lasts 3 minutes.
- The Jade Sea Fleet now finds frozen, magical forest, wasteland and ocean climates suitable.
- Abyssal Grand Cannon: each Cloudburst volley now uses 2 ammunition (shells per volley unchanged); Torrent is unaffected.
- Miko recruitment slots now open once the Shrine reaches tier 3.

### Fixed
- The Kirin summoned by Sayo was missing Thunder Echo and its heavenly lightning.
- The Alchemist could not be recruited after the fleet Watchtower was upgraded to level 2.
