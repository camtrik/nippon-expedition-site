首次上线创意工坊。沈长风率玉海舰队远征尼朋，在极东之海寻找立足之地。

### 新阵营与角色
- 新增可玩震旦派系 **「玉海舰队」**
- 传奇领主 **『无冕提督』沈长风**：专属游牧系统，可使用深渊系与阳系法术，拥有『云帆号跃迁』
- 三位传奇英雄（经远征同伴系统招募）：**『战鬼』立花直虎**、**『阴阳之巫女』纱代**、**瑟兰娜**
- 通用领主 **武士大将**：为尼朋兵种提供独特加成，可骑乘麒麟
- 通用英雄 **巫女**：使用全新的巫女系法术

### 远征同伴系统
专属「远征同伴」面板，召集三位同伴、推进各自的章节任务链、提升羁绊等级。

- 四档羁绊：结缘 → 信任 → 托付 → 生死之交，每档解锁对应部队与加成
- 名望：在面板中用名望购买已解锁的部队
- 三条独立章节任务链

### 兵种与建筑
- 新增 8 个兵种 —— 舰队：玉海潮勇、潮汐禁卫、舰队铳手；尼朋：长枪武士、铁炮武士、雷刃旗本、麒麟、稻荷灵狐使者
- **旗舰建筑树**：玉海舰队的旗舰本身即一条建筑树
- **尼朋地标 5 处**：引雷之峰、輝山锻冶场、枪术道场、神兽宫、灵道社殿
- **尼朋建筑树**：神社链（稻荷神社 → 稻荷大社 → 灵兽神宫）与侍所链（侍所 → 旗本番）

### 胜利目标
短期胜利「立足尼朋」，以及两条长期路线 —— 路线 A「海外帝国」、路线 B「天朝守护」。

### 地图重组与 AI 强化
- 自动搬家 7 名原版传奇领主到尼朋及周边（格里姆格·铁皮、『流放者』斯卡布兰德、埃皮德穆斯、纳卡里、司库克领主、拉卡斯、『屠灭者』斯卡拉格），可在 MCT 中关闭
- 搬家领主与岛上氏族（奥本氏、山陽氏）的 AI 强度抬到原版头部传奇势力水平；两家首领改为武士大将并附带不朽，首府开局即 4 级主城自带旗本番

### 子 mod：周边 AI 强化
同步上线 **「远征尼朋 · 周边 AI 强化」** 子 mod，对整体 AI 以及尼朋、震旦周边的 AI 做进一步强化。

### 前置需求
需要 [Immortal Empires Expanded (IEE)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3007996493) 与 [Mixu's Unlocker (MIXER)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2859968660)；可选 [MCT](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2927955021)。

### 已知问题
- 与其他占用同一 MIXER 借位槽（`mixer_nip_nipponese_expedition`）的 mod 冲突
- 与其他改动尼朋地权 / 派系的 mod 可能冲突
- 反馈问题请附上：载入的 mod 列表、载入顺序，以及 `Warhammer3.exe` 同目录下的 `script_log_*.txt`

First Workshop release. Shen Changfeng leads the Jade Sea Fleet east to Nippon, looking for a foothold in the eastern sea.

### New faction and characters
- New playable Grand Cathay faction: **the Jade Sea Fleet**
- Legendary Lord **Shen Changfeng, the Uncrowned Admiral** — a nomadic faction with its own mechanics, casting from the Lore of the Deep and the Lore of Yang, with the Cloud Sails leap
- Three Legendary Heroes, recruited through the Expedition Companions system: **Tachibana Naotora, the Ghost**, **Sayo, the Onmyo Miko**, and **Serena**
- Generic lord **Samurai Taisho** — grants unique bonuses to Nipponese units, mountable on a Kirin
- Generic hero **Miko** — a shrine maiden with an entirely new Miko spell lore

### Expedition Companions
A dedicated Expedition Companions panel: muster the three companions, advance their chapter quests, raise their bond.

- Four bond tiers — Bound → Trusted → Sworn → Devoted — each unlocking that companion's units and bonuses
- Renown: spend it in the panel to buy the units you have unlocked
- Three separate chapter quest chains

### Units and buildings
- 8 new units — Fleet: Jade Sea Warriors, Tide Guard, Fleet Gunners; Nippon: Yari Samurai, Teppo Samurai, Thunderblade Hatamoto, Kirin, Inari Fox Emissary
- **Flagship building tree**: the Jade Sea Fleet's flagship is itself a building tree
- **Five Nippon landmarks**: Thunder-Drawing Peak, Hikariya Forge, Yari Dojo, Divine Beast Sanctum, Vim-To Shrine
- **Nippon building chains**: the shrine chain (Inari Shrine → Inari Grand Shrine → Divine Beast Sanctuary) and the samurai chain (Samurai Hall → Hatamoto Guard)

### Victory objectives
A short victory, "Foothold in Nippon", plus two long routes — Route A "Overseas Empire" and Route B "Bulwark of the Far East".

### Map reshuffle and AI buffs
- Relocates 7 vanilla legendary lords to Nippon and the lands around it (Grimgor Ironhide, Skarbrand the Exiled, Epidemius, N'Kari, Lord Skrolk, Rakarth, Skrag the Slaughterer) — switchable off in MCT
- The relocated lords and the island clans (Okumoto, Sanyo) are pushed up to top-tier vanilla legendary AI strength; both clan leaders become immortal Samurai Taisho, and their capitals start as tier-4 cities with a Hatamoto Guard already built

### Submod: Stronger AI
The **Nippon Expedition · Stronger AI** submod ships alongside this release, buffing the AI overall and around Nippon and Grand Cathay in particular.

### Requirements
Requires [Immortal Empires Expanded (IEE)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3007996493) and [Mixu's Unlocker (MIXER)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2859968660). [MCT](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2927955021) is optional.

### Known issues
- Conflicts with other mods using the same borrowed MIXER slot (`mixer_nip_nipponese_expedition`)
- May conflict with other mods that change Nippon's ownership or factions
- When reporting a problem, please include your mod list, load order, and the `script_log_*.txt` next to `Warhammer3.exe`
