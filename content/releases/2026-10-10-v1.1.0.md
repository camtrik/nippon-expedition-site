### 热修（2026-10-10）
- 奥本氏开局军队加强。
- 修复伊吹序章战斗胜利后不结束的问题。
- 修复长枪武士、铁炮武士、雷刃旗本随机偏色的问题。
- 巫女「神隐」特效修改。
- 局势调整：
    - 山陽氏开局军队调整。
    - 清宝天尊信徒开局拥有数量更多的军队。
- 玉海潮勇数值调整。

### 新增
- 新增传奇英雄『半妖行者』伊吹，可通过远征同伴系统招募。
- 新增兵种晓行众（太刀）、晓行众（弓）与国崩巨鬼，以及地标晓行之里。
- 沈长风的旗舰建筑栏位由 11 个增加到 12 个，新增旗舰建筑「晓行寮」。
- 新增开场动画和派系选择动画。
- 领主与英雄的技能树新增「登峰造极」，用来放点满后多余的技能点。
- 水师提督的技能树新增「兵法韬略」，解锁全军先锋部署。

### 调整
- 部分兵牌与插画重绘。
- 舷侧齐射：每发炮弹伤害 68 → 50，冷却 60 → 120 秒，且战斗一开始即可使用。
- 沧溟巨炮的弹种平衡重做：
    - 「奔流」直射弹命中后不再空爆散矢，改为纯动能直击；直击伤害 110/365 → 90/360，射程 450 → 400。
    - 「骤雨」空爆：母弹射程 450 → 400、破甲 33 → 27、落点散布加大；空爆半径 7 → 6、空爆破甲 35 → 20。
    - 水矢落地爆大幅下调：半径 5 → 3，单矢伤害 12+11 → 5+2。
- 云霄禁卫：招募费 2200 → 1900，维持费 550 → 475。
- 舰队铳手：子弹伤害 6+18 → 5+17（基础+破甲），可以伤害载具；维持费 135 → 150。
- 武士大将的战马重新建模，麒麟的铠甲重新建模。
- 开局调整：『流放者』斯卡布兰德的搬家落点改到海門。

### 修复
- 修复多人联机不同步的问题。

### Hotfix (2026-10-10)
- The Okumoto Clan starts with stronger armies.
- Fixed Ibuki's prologue battle not ending after a victory.
- Fixed Yari Samurai, Teppo Samurai and Thunderblade Hatamoto showing random colours.
- Spirited Away, from the Miko lore, has revised visual effects.
- Starting situation:
    - The Sanyo Clan's starting armies have been adjusted.
    - The Cult of Tsien-Tsin starts with more armies.
- Jade Sea Warriors: stats adjusted.

### Added
- New Legendary Hero: **Ibuki, the Hanyo Wanderer**, recruited through the Expedition Companions system.
- New units Akatsuki-shu (Katana), Akatsuki-shu (Bow) and the Kunikuzushi Oni, plus a new landmark, Dawnbreakers' Haven.
- Shen Changfeng's flagship goes from 11 building slots to 12, with a new flagship building: the Dawnbreakers' Quarters.
- New opening cinematic and faction-selection animation.
- Lords' and heroes' skill trees gain **Beyond the Summit**, a place for the skill points left over once everything else is maxed out.
- The Fleet Admiral's skill tree gains **The Art of War**, which unlocks Vanguard Deployment for the whole army.

### Changed
- Some unit cards and illustrations have been repainted.
- Broadside: damage per shell 68 → 50, cooldown 60 → 120 seconds, and it is now ready from the start of the battle.
- The Abyssal Grand Cannon's ammunition has been rebalanced:
    - Torrent's direct-fire shot no longer bursts into a spray of bolts on impact and is now a pure kinetic hit; direct-hit damage 110/365 → 90/360, range 450 → 400.
    - Cloudburst airburst: carrier-shell range 450 → 400, armour-piercing 33 → 27, wider landing spread; airburst radius 7 → 6, airburst armour-piercing 35 → 20.
    - The water bolts' ground bursts are much weaker: radius 5 → 3, damage per bolt 12+11 → 5+2.
- Empyrean Guard: recruitment cost 2200 → 1900, upkeep 550 → 475.
- Fleet Gunners: bullet damage 6+18 → 5+17 (base + armour-piercing), and they can now damage vehicles; upkeep 135 → 150.
- The Samurai Taisho's warhorse has a new model, and so does the Kirin's armour.
- Campaign start: Skarbrand the Exiled now relocates to Kaimon.

### Fixed
- Fixed multiplayer desyncs.
