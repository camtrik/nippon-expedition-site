### 新增
- 玉海舰队现在可以通过任务解锁**长牙之路**。任务目标：占领黎山或玉江三角洲，或与其控制者结为军事同盟。
- 新增可选子 mod **「Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun 兼容包」**。与 [Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3770643554) 同时开启时，两个 mod 不再互相顶掉传奇领主——玉海舰队改用另一个空阵营位，把原来那个整个让给对方。
    - ⚠️ 装上兼容包后，此前的玉海舰队存档会读不出来。不装的玩家不受影响。
- 其他震旦势力占下尼朋的城之后，现在也能建造当地的地标。
- 其他震旦势力可以通过占领**竜舰海港**解锁侍所、占领**橙色神華大社**解锁神社，开局会收到对应任务；解锁之后不再受地域限制。

### 修复
- 修复沈长风的军队没有魔法之风储量、每场战斗开局魔风极少的问题。
- 修复巫女与纱代技能树倒数第二排那两格属性技能，点满第二级后属性不再增长的问题。
- 修复瑟兰娜骑银月龙时不会喷吐「银月龙吐息」的问题；并补上步行、披甲伊瑟拉玛银战马、伊瑟拉玛银战车三个形态缺失的高等精灵被动「尚武造诣」。
- 修复其他 mod 给震旦新增的建筑与地标在玉海舰队这里一个都不出现的问题。现在其他 mod 给震旦加的新建筑，玉海舰队也能照常建造。
- 修复武者屋形与舰上稻荷社在同伴尚未入队时，锁定提示显示为空白的问题。

### Added
- The Jade Sea Fleet can now unlock **the Ivory Road** through a mission. Objective: take Mount Li or the Jade River Delta, or form a military alliance with whoever holds them.
- New optional submod: **the Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun compatibility pack**. With [Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3770643554) enabled alongside it, the two mods no longer kill each other's legendary lord — the Jade Sea Fleet moves to a different empty faction slot and leaves the original one entirely to the other mod.
    - ⚠️ Installing the compat pack makes existing Jade Sea Fleet saves unreadable. Players who don't install it are unaffected.
- Other Grand Cathay factions can now build Nippon's landmarks in Nipponese settlements they have taken.
- Other Grand Cathay factions can unlock the Samurai Hall by taking **Dragon Fleet Port** and the shrine chain by taking the **Temple of the Orange Simca**; they receive the matching missions at campaign start, and the buildings are no longer region-locked once unlocked.

### Fixed
- Shen Changfeng's armies had no Winds of Magic reserve and started every battle with almost none.
- The two attribute skills on the second-to-last row of the Miko's and Sayo's skill trees stopped granting attributes past the second point.
- Serena would not use Moon Dragon Breath while mounted on the Moon Dragon. Her on-foot, Barded Ithilmar Steed and Ithilmar Chariot forms were also missing the High Elf passive Martial Prowess.
- Buildings and landmarks added to Grand Cathay by other mods did not appear at all for the Jade Sea Fleet. New Cathay buildings from other mods are now buildable as normal.
- The Warrior's Manor and the Shipboard Inari Shrine showed a blank lock tooltip while their companion had not yet joined.
