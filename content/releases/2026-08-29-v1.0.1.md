### 新增
- 玉海舰队现在可以通过任务解锁**长牙之路**。任务目标：占领黎山或玉江三角洲，或以军事同盟控制其中之一。
- 新增可选子 mod **「Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun 兼容包」**。与 [Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3770643554) 同时开启时，两个 mod 不再互相顶掉传奇领主。
    - ⚠️ 需要新开存档。
- 其他震旦势力占下尼朋的城之后，现在也能建造当地的地标。
- 其他震旦势力可以通过占领**竜舰海港**解锁侍所与武士大将的招募、占领**橙色神華大社**解锁神社与巫女的招募，开局会收到对应任务；解锁之后不再受地域限制。
- 由 AI 操作的玉海舰队，会在开局及此后的战役进程中陆续自动生成瑟兰娜、立花直虎、纱代三位传奇英雄。

### 修复
- 修复沈长风的军队没有魔法之风储量、每场战斗开局魔风极少的问题。
- 修复巫女与纱代技能树倒数第二排那两格属性技能，点满第二级后属性不再增长的问题。
- 修复瑟兰娜骑银月龙时不会喷吐「银月龙吐息」的问题；并补上步行、披甲伊瑟拉玛银战马、伊瑟拉玛银战车三个形态缺失的高等精灵被动「尚武造诣」。
- 修复其他 mod 给震旦新增的建筑与地标在玉海舰队这里一个都不出现的问题。现在其他 mod 给震旦加的新建筑，玉海舰队也能照常建造。
- 修复武者屋形与舰上稻荷社在同伴尚未入队时，锁定提示显示为空白的问题。
- 补上纱代与瑟兰娜缺失的默认不朽。

### Added
- The Jade Sea Fleet can now unlock **the Ivory Road** through a mission. Objective: take Mount Li or the Jade River Delta, or hold one of them through a military alliance.
- New optional submod: **the Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun compatibility pack**. With [Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3770643554) enabled alongside it, the two mods no longer overwrite each other's legendary lord.
    - ⚠️ Requires a new save.
- Other Grand Cathay factions can now build Nippon's landmarks in Nipponese settlements they have taken.
- Other Grand Cathay factions can unlock the Samurai Hall and Samurai Taisho recruitment by taking **Dragon Fleet Port**, and the Shrine chain and Miko recruitment by taking the **Temple of the Orange Simca**; they receive the matching missions at campaign start, and neither stays region-locked once unlocked.
- An AI-controlled Jade Sea Fleet now spawns its three Legendary Heroes — Serena, Tachibana Naotora and Sayo — at campaign start and over the course of the campaign.

### Fixed
- Shen Changfeng's armies had no Winds of Magic reserve and started every battle with almost none.
- The two attribute skills on the second-to-last row of the Miko's and Sayo's skill trees stopped granting attributes past the second point.
- Serena would not use Moon Dragon Breath while mounted on the Moon Dragon. Her on-foot, Barded Ithilmar Steed and Ithilmar Chariot forms were also missing the High Elf passive Martial Prowess.
- Buildings and landmarks added to Grand Cathay by other mods did not appear at all for the Jade Sea Fleet. New Cathay buildings from other mods are now buildable as normal.
- The Warrior's Manor and the Shipboard Inari Shrine showed a blank lock tooltip while their companion had not yet joined.
- Sayo and Serena were missing the immortality they are meant to have by default.
