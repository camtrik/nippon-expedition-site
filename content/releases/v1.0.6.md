### 调整
- 武士大将模型更新，拥有三个不同的变体与对应兵牌。
- 巫女法系「神隐」获得新特效。

### 修复
- 修复同伴进度变化时容易闪退的问题。
- 修复大社地标升级为灵道本殿后，还能再建一座灵道拜殿的问题。
- 修复武士配色：主要派系的长枪武士固定为红色，铁炮武士固定为黑色。

### Changed
- The Samurai Taisho has a new model, with three distinct variants and a unit card for each.
- Spirited Away, from the Miko lore, has new visual effects.

### Fixed
- Fixed a crash that could happen when a companion's progress changed.
- After the landmark at The Great Shrine was upgraded to the Great Shrine Inner Sanctuary, a second Great Shrine Outer Sanctuary could still be built.
- Samurai colours: for the major factions, Yari Samurai are now always red and Teppo Samurai always black.
