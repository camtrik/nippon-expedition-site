### 热修（2026-08-30）
- 沈长风的「震旦航海术」加强：贸易货物收益由 +10% 提高到 +20%，并新增港口产值 +10%。
- 沈长风的「玉海剑圣」新增战场主动技能「玉海剑舞」。
- 凌涛坐骑的战场技能「潮随刃动」加强：伤害由 20 提高到 33（33% 破甲），冷却由 25 秒缩短到 15 秒。

### 新增
- 尼朋的建筑与地标现在会提供驻军。
- 侍所链与武者屋形链各新增一级入门建筑，长枪武士现在在 2 级定居点就能招募。

### 调整
- 雷刃旗本与麒麟的招募回合数由 1 回合增加到 2 回合。

### Hotfix (2026-08-30)
- Shen Changfeng's Cathayan Seamanship is stronger: trade goods income raised from +10% to +20%, plus a new +10% to port income.
- Her Jade Sea Swordmaster skill now grants the Jade Blade Dance battle ability.
- Tide Follows the Blade, on the Tidestrider mount, hits harder: damage goes from 20 to 33 (33% armour-piercing) and its cooldown from 25 seconds to 15.

### Added
- Nippon's buildings and landmarks now provide garrison units.
- The Samurai Hall and Warrior's Manor chains each gain an entry-level building, so Yari Samurai can be recruited from a level 2 settlement.

### Changed
- Thunderblade Hatamoto and Kirin now take 2 turns to recruit, up from 1.
