### 新增
- 新增可选子 mod **[「震旦机制兼容包」](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3792252212)**。给原版震旦加机制的 mod 大多按阵营发放，名单里没有玉海舰队就用不上；这个包负责把玉海舰队补进名单，之后会继续接入更多这类 mod。
- 首个接入 **[五行罗盘大修](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3727513610)**：玉海舰队现在也能用上那个 mod 的新罗盘。
    - 需要同时订阅那个 mod，只装兼容包不生效。

<!-- lang: en -->

### Added
- New optional submod: **[the Cathay Mechanics Compatibility Patch](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3792252212)**. Mods that add mechanics to vanilla Grand Cathay usually grant them faction by faction, and the Jade Sea Fleet is not on those lists; this patch puts it there, and more such mods will be added over time.
- First one covered: **[Cathay Compass Overhaul](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3727513610)** — the Jade Sea Fleet can now use that mod's new compass.
    - Requires a subscription to that mod as well; the patch on its own does nothing.
