<!-- lang: zh -->

这个 mod 只有同时订阅了 **[Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3770643554)** 的玩家才需要装。

- 2026-08-30 **NRS 的尼朋兵种现在可以在岸上招募了**：足轻、武士、骑马武士、武僧、忍者、符咒巨人、火炮等，拥有独特的建筑树。
    - 这些兵种可以享受震旦领主技能与科技的加成。
    - 玉海舰队与岛上的尼朋派系开局即可招募；其他震旦派系开局会收到任务，攻占对应建筑后解锁。
    - NRS 的领主与英雄暂不开放招募——它们的战役技能线还不完备。
- 2026-08-30 修复定居点在战役地图上不显示建筑模型的问题。原版震旦的城市、地图上的特殊定居点、乃至 NRS 自己的尼朋城市都受影响。
- 2026-08-30 修复震旦系派系攻占城市后没有任何占领选项的问题。NRS 自己的尼朋选项原样保留，两边互不影响。
- 2026-08-29 首次兼容：玉海舰队改用另一个阵营位，两个 mod 不再互相顶掉传奇领主，双方派系可以同时出现在战役里。
    - ⚠️ 装上本包之前存的玉海舰队存档会读不出来。不装本包的玩家不受影响。

<!-- lang: en -->

You only need this mod if you are also subscribed to **[Nippon, Land of the Rising Sun](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3770643554)**.

- 2026-08-30 **NRS's Nippon units can now be recruited on shore**: Ashigaru, samurai, mounted samurai, warrior monks, ninjas, fuju giants, artillery and more, with a dedicated building tree.
    - These units benefit from Cathay lord skills and technologies.
    - The Jade Sea Fleet and the Nippon factions on the island can recruit them from the start; other Cathay factions receive a mission at campaign start and unlock them by capturing the corresponding building.
    - NRS's lords and heroes are not available for recruitment yet — their campaign skill trees are not complete.
- 2026-08-30 Fixed settlements showing no building models on the campaign map. Vanilla Cathay cities, the map's special settlements and even NRS's own Nippon cities were all affected.
- 2026-08-30 Fixed Cathay factions having no occupation options after capturing a city. NRS's own Nippon options are kept as they are, and neither side affects the other.
- 2026-08-29 First compatibility pass: the Jade Sea Fleet moves to a different faction slot, so the two mods no longer overwrite each other's legendary lord and both factions can appear in the same campaign.
    - ⚠️ Jade Sea Fleet saves made before installing this patch will no longer load. Players who do not install it are unaffected.
